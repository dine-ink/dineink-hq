Internal & Pitch Reference

Everything DineInk does, and why it's there

DineInk runs the full back-of-house and front-of-house operation for a restaurant — from the day a new branch is set up, through billing, kitchen throughput, inventory, staffing, and the financial reporting an owner needs to know if the business is actually profitable. This guide covers every real, working feature in the app: what it does, and why a restaurant owner would use it.

Getting Started

Guided Onboarding

The setup a new restaurant walks through before going live — one guided flow instead of piecing configuration together across scattered screens.

5-Step Setup Wizard

A progress-tracked wizard walks a new owner through Restaurant Details (name, GST number, logo), Branch Setup (address, table count with auto-generated tables), Menu Setup (categories with icons, items with price/prep time/veg-or-non-veg), Billing Settings (GST%, service charge, payment methods, which billing modes are active), and Staff Setup (roles, departments, shifts, login access).

Gets a restaurant — even a multi-branch one — fully configured and operational in one sitting, rather than requiring an owner to hunt through six different settings screens before they can take a single order.

Shown automatically to new accounts

Multi-Branch Setup From Day One

An owner can add more than one branch during setup, each with its own address, table count, and billing configuration — with the option to copy one branch's billing setup to another or apply it to all branches at once.

Restaurant groups don't have to repeat identical configuration work branch by branch.

Onboarding → Branch Setup

Front of House

Dashboard

The home screen after login — a live snapshot of how the business is doing right now, for the selected branch and date range.

Live KPI Strip

Six headline numbers: total revenue, completed orders, average order value, unique customers, the single busiest hour, and EBITDA with margin %.

Everything an owner needs to check "how's business today" without opening a single report.

Dashboard → top of page

Revenue & Orders Charts

A trend chart of revenue and a bar chart of order counts over the selected range — hourly for a single day, daily across a longer window.

Spot sales trends, slow periods, or spikes at a glance, and see whether a revenue change is driven by more orders or bigger tickets.

Dashboard → charts

Order Channel Split

A donut chart of dine-in vs. online order share.

Shows channel mix for staffing and marketing decisions.

Dashboard

Payment Method Split

Revenue broken down by how customers actually paid (cash, card, UPI, etc.).

Useful for negotiating processor fees or planning terminal needs.

Dashboard

Top Selling Items & Categories

Ranked lists of the best-performing dishes and menu categories by quantity sold.

Informs promotion, stocking, and menu-design decisions.

Dashboard

Recent Orders

A table of the most recent bills — customer, order type, payment method, amount, status, date.

A quick audit trail without opening the full Bills page.

Front of House

Bills

Every transaction and every order still being prepared, in one searchable feed.

Unified Transaction Feed

Shows finalized bills and still-open kitchen orders together, sorted by recency, so nothing is hidden across two separate screens.

An owner or manager sees the complete picture — what's paid and what's still cooking — in one place.

Bills

Search & Filters

Filter by customer name, phone, payment method, order type, amount, or bill ID, and narrow to a date range.

Find one transaction fast instead of scrolling a long list.

Bills

Revenue & Bills Summary Cards

Total revenue, bill count, average bill, and paid-bill count for whatever is currently filtered — cancelled bills are excluded from the totals.

An accurate at-a-glance sales snapshot for any slice of the data an owner is looking at.

Bills

Bill Detail Drawer

Click any transaction to see the full itemized breakdown — every line item, plus subtotal, discount, GST, CGST/SGST, service charge, packing charge, and grand total.

Lets an owner audit exactly how any single bill's final amount was built.

Bills → click a row

Front of House

Customers

Who the restaurant's customers are, how much they're worth, and which ones need a reason to come back.

Overview

Customer Directory & Search

A searchable table of every customer for the branch — visit count, average bill, total spend, preferred order type, last visit.

A quick, searchable directory of who's spending and how much, without digging through raw order data.

Customers → Overview

Customer Segment Tags

Each customer is tagged VIP (spend over ₹5,000), Regular (more than 3 visits), or New.

Spot high-value customers in the list at a glance.

Customers → Overview

Churn Analysis

Active / At Risk / Churned Buckets

Every customer is automatically sorted by days since last visit: Active (within 30 days), At Risk (30–90 days), or Churned (90+ days).

See how many customers are drifting away before they're gone for good — without manual analysis.

Customers → Churn Analysis

Top 10 by Spend, At-Risk List, Churned List

Three ranked lists: your highest-value customers, customers 30–90 days quiet, and customers gone 90+ days — each with visit count, spend, and last-visit date.

Concrete, actionable lists for retention outreach or win-back campaigns, prioritized by who matters most.

Customers → Churn Analysis

RFM Scoring

Recency / Frequency / Monetary Segmentation

Every customer is scored 1–5 on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value, rolled up into five segments: Champion, Loyal, Potential, At Risk, Lost — with customer count and revenue per segment.

An industry-standard way to identify best customers, growth opportunities, and customers about to be lost — beyond simple spend sorting.

Customers → RFM Score

Front of House

Cash

A daily reconciliation report for the physical cash drawer — did what's counted match what the system expected?

Daily Cash Session Reconciliation

For each day's register session, shows who opened it, opening float, system-expected cash (opening + cash-only sales), the physically counted closing amount, and the difference.

Visibility into whether the drawer balanced every day, without needing to be there at close-out.

Cash

Discrepancy Alerts

A banner and per-row flag highlight any session where counted cash was off by more than ₹500, with a running total of shortfall vs. surplus.

Flags a variance large enough to warrant investigation — theft, a counting error, or unrecorded cash spend.

Cash

Cash Trend Charts

A line chart of the daily cash difference and a bar chart comparing opening vs. closing cash across the period.

Spot a recurring shortfall pattern (same day, same shift) rather than treating every variance as a one-off.

Cash

Kitchen & Inventory

Menu & Inventory Management

Listed in the sidebar as "Operations" — the control center for the menu itself, ingredient stock, recipe costing, and kitchen procedures.

Menu

Menu Item & Category Management

Create, edit, and delete menu items and categories — name, description, price, prep time, veg/non-veg type, and an availability toggle — plus sort and filter by price, type, and category.

The single source of truth for what's on the menu and what it costs the customer.

Operations → Menu

Ingredients

Ingredient Inventory

Every ingredient organized by category, with quantity on hand, unit, purchase price, computed price-per-unit, and a configurable reorder level.

One place to see and maintain the raw-material stock the kitchen runs on.

Operations → Ingredients

Vendor Assignment & Price History

Assign which vendor supplies each ingredient, and update an ingredient's price with a recorded, auditable history of every past change.

Know who to call when stock runs low, and keep a clean paper trail on cost changes over time.

Operations → Ingredients

Restock

Monthly Restock Ledger

A per-ingredient monthly record of opening stock, purchases, spend, and closing stock, with Excel template download and upload for offline data entry.

Keeps a running, exportable record of what came in and what it cost, month over month.

Operations → Restock

Item Mapping

Recipe Costing

Map exact ingredient quantities to each dish and instantly see that dish's recipe cost, profit margin, and food-cost percentage — with AI-assisted ingredient suggestions to speed up mapping a new dish.

Answers the question every menu-pricing decision depends on: what does this dish actually cost to make?

Operations → Item Mapping

Analytics

Ingredient Consumption Analytics

See which ingredients are consumed the most and which cost the most, filterable by ingredient category.

Surfaces exactly where inventory spend is concentrated.

Operations → Analytics

Operational Alerts

Real-time alerts for low stock (with a one-click "Restock from [Vendor]" action straight to a purchase invoice), stock mismatches, over-portioning (a dish's food cost running too high), and a reminder to complete the daily stock audit once closing time has passed.

Puts the problems that need attention today in front of the owner, instead of requiring them to go looking.

Operations → Analytics

Operations (SOP)

SOP Checklists

Standard operating procedures — prep steps, portioning standards, hygiene checklists — written as ordered checklists, optionally linked to a specific dish.

Keeps quality and consistency documented and repeatable, rather than living only in one chef's head.

Operations → Operations tab

Vendor Directory

Create and manage vendor contact records — name, phone, email, address.

A single, organized list of every supplier the restaurant works with.

Vendors

Payments & Invoice Tracking

Record purchase invoices and payments per vendor, with outstanding balance tracked automatically.

Know exactly what's owed to which supplier without cross-referencing paper receipts.

Vendors → vendor detail

Ingredients Supplied View

Answers "what do we buy from this vendor, and at what price?" without leaving the vendor's page.

Kitchen & Inventory

Daily Stock Audit

The end-of-day ritual that turns raw stock counts into a real wastage number.

End-of-Day Closing Stock Entry

Staff enter the actual counted closing quantity for each ingredient at the end of the day, with a field for notes.

Captures the ground truth needed to know what was actually used versus what should have been used.

Stock Audit

Automatic Wastage Calculation

Compares opening stock minus the expected consumption (from recipes × dishes actually sold) against the real closing count, to compute wastage automatically.

Turns a manual stock count into an immediate wastage figure — no spreadsheet math required.

Stock Audit

Kitchen

How fast the kitchen is actually running, broken down by hour, order type, and table.

Kitchen KPI Summary

Total completed orders, average completion time, SLA compliance %, fastest order, and peak hour, for the selected range.

An instant read on kitchen speed and whether it's hitting its own service target.

Kitchen

Throughput & Speed Charts

Orders completed by hour, average completion time by hour, a daily completion trend, and speed compared across order types.

Pinpoints exactly when and where the kitchen slows down, so staffing can be adjusted to match.

Kitchen

Table Turn Rate

For dine-in orders, each table's order count and average completion time, tagged Fast, Normal, or Slow.

Identifies which tables or sections are a bottleneck for seating throughput.

Kitchen

Top Items & Slowest Orders

The most frequently prepared dishes, and a list of the slowest completed orders with an SLA met/missed flag.

Supports prep planning for high-volume dishes, and gives concrete outliers to investigate rather than just an average.

People

Attendance

Who's working, when, and what it's costing — by day and by month.

Daily Attendance Register

Clock-in/out times, break minutes, hours worked, an estimated daily wage, and a Present/Late/Absent status for every staff member, for any selected date.

A single daily view of who showed up and for how long, without a physical register.

Attendance

Department & Monthly Trend Charts

A chart comparing total vs. present staff per department, and a monthly line chart of daily headcount against the full-team benchmark.

Spot understaffed departments on a given day, or recurring attendance dips across the month.

Attendance

Staff Productivity

Month-to-date labour totals, revenue generated by shift window, a department-by-department cost breakdown, and a per-staff efficiency table with attendance rate and cost-per-hour.

Rolls up labour cost and utilization for the month in one place, and flags which staff have low attendance or high effective cost.

Attendance → Productivity

Business Intelligence

Insights

Where an owner tells the app about their real costs, so profitability numbers are grounded in reality, not just revenue.

Profitability Overview

Revenue, net profit, EBITDA %, and prime cost % (food + labour as a share of revenue), plus a tracker comparing current EBITDA against a self-set target.

Turns raw revenue into the profitability question that actually matters: is the restaurant making money after real costs?

Insights → Overview

Revenue Targets Planner

Shows the revenue needed to hit each of several EBITDA benchmarks (0–25%), given current expenses.

Directly answers "how much more do I need to sell to hit X% profitability?"

Insights → Overview

Cost Breakdown & Food Cost Tracking

Fixed, variable, labour, tax, and raw-material costs as a share of the month's expenses, with food-cost % flagged if it climbs over 35%.

Shows an owner exactly where money is going, and surfaces food cost — one of the most important health metrics in the business — without spreadsheet math.

Insights → Setup → Raw Material Cost

Labour Intelligence

Total labour cost, average salary, department-by-department salary breakdown, and a full staff roster with role, salary, and shift — pulled directly from real staff records.

Gives labour-cost visibility tied to actual headcount, not an estimate.

Insights → Setup → Labour

Financial Targets & Tax/Finance Setup

Set target percentages for EBITDA, food cost, gross margin, and prime cost, and record GST, loan EMI, interest, accounting fees, and insurance so they're included in every profitability calculation.

Makes sure financing and tax overhead are counted, not forgotten — and gives an owner reference benchmarks to set realistic goals against.

Insights → Setup
Note: Insights' profitability numbers (EBITDA, Net Profit, Prime Cost, Food Cost) are only as accurate as the expense and inventory figures an owner keeps up to date — this module is a calculator built on real revenue data plus the owner's own inputs, not a fully automatic accounting system.

Business Intelligence

Reports

Twelve report views, all filterable by date range and branch, covering financials, the menu, wastage, and demand patterns in depth.

Financial Reports

P&L Statement

A full income & expenditure statement — gross revenue down to net profit/loss — plus a revenue trend chart and a channel-split donut chart.

One page to see whether the restaurant is actually profitable after tax and logged expenses, not just how much revenue came in.

Reports → P&L Statement

Tax Report

CGST, SGST, and total GST collected, with a bill-by-bill breakdown and accurate period totals.

A ready reference for GST filing and reconciliation.

Reports → Tax Report

Expense Tracker

Total expenses by category, an expense-to-revenue ratio, and the most recent individual entries.

Shows where operating cash is going and whether costs are creeping up relative to sales.

Reports → Expense Tracker

Sales Analytics

Revenue broken down by payment method and by order channel (dine-in/takeaway/delivery), plus a paid-vs-unpaid summary.

Shows how customers are ordering and paying, and how much revenue is still uncollected.

Reports → Sales Analytics

Discount Analysis

Total discounts given, average discount per bill, and every discounted bill listed, with anything over 20% flagged.

Catches excessive discounting patterns that are invisible bill-by-bill on paper receipts.

Reports → Discount Analysis

Menu Intelligence

Menu Engineering Matrix

Classifies every dish into Star / Puzzle / Plowhorse / Dog based on sales volume and margin, with a specific tip per quadrant (promote, reprice, remove, etc.).

A classic, at-a-glance way to decide which dishes to push, fix, or drop from the menu.

Reports → Menu Engineering

Best Sellers & Most Profitable Dishes

A true sales-ranked best-sellers list with food-cost % and margin % side by side, plus separate rankings for most-profitable dish overall and by category.

Separates "sells the most" from "makes the most money" — two very different, both important, questions.

Reports → Menu Engineering

Category Food Cost

Average food cost % and margin % rolled up per menu category, with each category's top dish.

Shows which parts of the menu (not just individual dishes) are pulling margin down.

Reports → Menu Engineering

Compare Dishes & Suggested Pricing

Pick two dishes to compare cost, price, and margin side by side; every dish also shows a suggested selling price that would hit a 65% profit margin.

Makes repricing decisions concrete instead of guesswork — what should this actually cost to hit the margin we want?

Reports → Menu Engineering

Menu Split — Price High / Usage High

Two ranked views: the highest-priced items on the menu, and the highest-volume sellers.

Two different levers for menu strategy — premium positioning versus volume drivers.

Reports → Menu Engineering

Operational Reports

Table Analytics

Revenue and average turn time per table, with a High/Low performance badge.

Shows which tables generate the most revenue and which sit occupied too long.

Reports → Table Analytics

Waste Report & Stock Lifecycle

Logged wastage/damage/expiry entries with cost attached, plus a full per-ingredient wastage lifecycle (opening → purchases → consumption → closing) viewable by weight, by product, or by price.

Wastage against SOP-expected consumption, broken down every way an owner would need to act on it — how much, of what, and what it cost.

Reports → Waste Report / Stock Lifecycle

Hourly Heatmap

Revenue and order volume by hour of day and day of week, with an item/category filter to see when a specific dish or category is in highest demand.

Answers "when should we staff up" both restaurant-wide and for a specific menu category.

Reports → Hourly Heatmap

Day Analysis

Revenue, orders, and average bill by day of week, with Peak/Busy/Slow labels.

Plan staffing, promotions, or prep around real weekly demand patterns.

Reports → Day Analysis

Revenue Forecast

A 7-day-ahead revenue projection based on recent trend, with a daily breakdown and expected range.

A lightweight, forward-looking number for cash-flow, staffing, and purchasing decisions.

Reports → Revenue Forecast

Business Intelligence

Compare

For multi-branch operators — put every location side by side and see who's actually winning.

Branch vs. Branch / City vs. City

Toggle the entire comparison between individual outlets and cities (branches grouped by shared city).

Analyze performance at whichever level matches how the business is actually organized.

Compare

Winner Cards & Full Metric Table

Highlights which location has the highest revenue, most orders, best average bill, and most profit — then a full table of Revenue, Orders, Avg Bill, Discounts, GST, Expenses, Net Profit, and Staff across every location, with the best performer per row flagged.

Immediately surfaces the best and worst performing location without reading a full table row by row — then gives the detail to back it up.

Compare

Order Mix & Payment Preference by Location

Order-type breakdown and payment-method mix, shown per branch/city.

Reveals whether one location's business runs differently — more delivery, more cash — informing local decisions.

Compare

Configuration

Settings & Shops

Restaurant, branch, table, and staff configuration — everything an owner needs to keep the operation's setup accurate as it grows.

Restaurant Profile

The organization-wide name, contact details, GST number, and logo — separate from any individual branch's own information.

A single, consistent identity used across the whole account.

Settings

Branch Management

Add, edit, close, or reopen branches — address, contact details, and a closing time used to trigger the daily stock audit reminder.

Central place to onboard a new outlet or adjust an existing one's details and hours.

Settings → Branches, or Shops

Table & Staff Management

Per branch: manage the table/seating list (name, capacity), and the staff directory (role, department, shift, salary, login access).

Keeps floor plan and headcount records accurate per location.

Shops

Per-Branch Billing Configuration

Toggle which billing modes are active (table-wise, quick billing, takeaway, delivery, QR ordering, KOT), set GST % and service charge %, and choose accepted payment methods — independently per branch.

Different locations can legitimately run different tax rates or accept different payment types.
